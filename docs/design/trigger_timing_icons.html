Tradeoffs

Set A needs no tutorial — sword and shield are universal, and they echo the game's attack/defend vocabulary directly. The cost: the sword and sunburst carry fine detail that softens below ~14 px, and a shield glyph could be misread as "this card is good at defending" rather than "fires when defending".

Set B stays crisp at any size — pure strokes, no fine detail — and reads as timing notation rather than a combat stat. The cost: players must learn the marks once (a line in the how-to-play or a tooltip covers it), and the ring is the least self-evident of the six.
