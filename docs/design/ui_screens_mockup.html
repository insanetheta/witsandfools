Implementation Notes

  • Card reward appears after ShowResult() and before ability pick (ShowAbilityPick())

  • Offer 3 cards with weighted rarity: Common 60%, Uncommon 30%, Rare 10%. Elite/Boss matches increase Rare chance to 25%
