Art Direction Notes

Ability cards use rarity border colors: bronze (common), silver (uncommon), gold (rare). Type badges are color-coded: red for attack, blue for defense, amber for utility. The hovered card rises and its border glows. Victory text fades in with gold particles rising from below.
