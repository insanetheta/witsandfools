Game Overview

Wits & Fools is a single-player roguelike card game set in Renaissance Italy. The player selects an archetype, then travels a branching map through five increasingly prestigious venues, playing Durak-variant card matches against colorful opponents. Between matches, the player builds a deck, acquires abilities and trinkets, and manages an economy of Florins and Prestige.

The card game core is derived from Durak (Russian "fool"), where the attacker plays cards and the defender must beat each attack with a higher card of the same suit or any trump card. The defender can choose to "eat" (accept) attacks rather than defend them, adding those cards to their hand. The first player to empty their hand (when the draw deck is exhausted) wins.

Attack & Defense

A match consists of multiple bouts. Each bout, one player attacks and the other defends. Players alternate as attacker each bout.

Attack Rules

  • First attack card in a bout: any card is legal
  • Follow-up attacks: card rank must match a rank already in play (on the table)
  • Attacker can stop attacking at any point (voluntarily ending the bout)
  • Maximum 6 attacks per bout (increased by PileOn ability and RelentlessAssault ascension)
  • Duelist's Glove trinket: first attack ignores rank-match restriction
  • DoubleTrouble ability: bypass rank-match for one attack
  • AnyRankAttack house rule: disables rank-match entirely

Defense Rules

  • Defender must play a card that beats the attack card
  • Same suit: higher rank wins (e.g., 8♥ beats 7♥)
  • Trump suit: any trump beats any non-trump (e.g., 6♠ trump beats Ace♥ non-trump)
  • Trump vs trump: higher rank wins normally
  • EndgameSpecialist passive: when deck ≤ 6 cards, defend with any suit
  • HereticsBrand trinket: opponent's trump treated as 1 rank lower

Trump Suit

One suit is designated trump at the start of each match (random unless overridden). Trump cards beat all non-trump cards regardless of rank.

  • TrumpChanger ability: change trump suit once per game
  • CrownSeize ability (3 Fury): change trump and draw 2 cards
  • AlchemistsStone trinket: choose trump suit at match start
  • NoTrumpsUntilBout house rule: restricts trump attacks until specified bout number
  • GracefulManners passive: gain 2 resource when trump changes
  • TrumpAffinity passive: draw extra card when drawing trump cards

Eating (Accepting Attacks)

Instead of defending, the defender can eat all undefended attack cards, adding them to their hand. This ends the bout.

  • All undefended cards go into the defender's hand
  • PoisonedWine trinket: when opponent eats, they draw 2 extra cards
  • EatDrawsExtra house rule: eating draws 2 extra cards
  • ThickSkin passive: eater draws 1 extra card
  • Bloodlust passive: attacker gains 1 Fury per card eaten by opponent
  • BruteFury perk: attacker draws 1 and gains 1 Fury when opponent eats
  • GracefulRetreat passive: eat first card to discard pile instead of hand
  • LuckyDraw perk: after eating, discard worst card (once per match)
  • DesperationDiscard: if hand ≥ 10 after eating, discard 2 worst cards and gain 1 resource

Bout Flow

SETUP → Draw hands (6 cards each) → Set trump suit ↓ BOUT START → Passive triggers (Equilibrium, SteadyHand, SharkInstinct, MarkedCards) ↓ ATTACK PHASE → Attacker plays card(s) → Triggers fire (OnAttack abilities) ↓ DEFENSE PHASE → Defender plays card or eats → Triggers fire (OnDefend abilities) ↓ RESOLVING → Bout outcome determined → Cards discarded or eaten ↓ ↓ ↓ Resource decay applied ↓ Both draw back to hand size ↓ NEXT BOUT → Attacker/Defender swap → Repeat until... ↓ GAME OVER → First player to empty hand (with empty deck) wins
Win Condition
The game ends when the draw decks are exhausted and one player empties their hand. That player wins. The loser (the "fool") is the one left holding cards.
