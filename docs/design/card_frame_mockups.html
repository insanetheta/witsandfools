Card Frame — Three Directions

The current cards paste the portrait into a plain rectangle between two flat bands — rank, name and ability read as stickers on top. These three frames each integrate the art and the UI into one object, in the Venetian / candlelit-Caravaggio theme.

Same five cards in every design (Courtesan 7♥ · Gladiator K♥ · Poisoner 6♠ · Dragon's Hoard A♣ · Transmuter J♦) so you can compare directly. Real generated art.

A · Gilt Cartouche

The portrait as a framed gallery painting

The art lives inside a carved-gold picture frame (beveled, inset-shadowed) — it reads as a hung Renaissance portrait, not a pasted image. The rank is a struck-gold coin in the corner, the name is an engraved nameplate overlapping the frame's base, and the ability sits on a recessed parchment shelf. Richest / most ornate; best on larger surfaces (reward, deck view, card detail).

STATES — The Courtesan

B · Full-Bleed Cinematic

The art is the card; UI floats on candlelight scrims

The portrait fills the whole card edge-to-edge (no window, nothing to look "slapped on"). A soft dark gradient rises from the bottom so the name + ability sit on the art legibly, and the rank is a corner gem. Gold filigree corners + a thin inner gold line tie it to the theme. Reads cleanly even at hand size — best for the in-match cards and the cleanest fit with the new felt board.

STATES — The Courtesan (playable glow · trump defense · rank bonus)

C · Venetian Engraving

A hand-printed 16th-century playing/tarot card

Aged parchment with a printed double-rule border and corner flourishes; the portrait sits in an arched niche and is lightly sepia-toned so it reads as an engraving rather than a photo drop-in. Rank as struck numerals in opposing corners, name on a ruled plaque, ability on a heraldic banner in the doctrine's colour. Most "period" and tactile; the warmest tonal match to the parchment map & event screens.

STATES — The Courtesan

How states attach (all three): playable = gold outer glow; trump defense = red “♥ TRUMP” tab at the card foot; rank bonus = red “+N” coin at the top-right; dimmed/unplayable = desaturate + 60% brightness. Doctrine is the small colour accent (Schemer teal · Brute red · Trickster violet · Hoarder amber); rarity tints the rank coin / gem (Common bronze · Uncommon steel · Rare gold).

Suggestion: B for the in-match hand (clean at small size, fits the felt), A for reward / deck / detail (room for ornament), with C as an alternate skin if we want a more antique whole-game tone. They can share one builder — the difference is the frame layer, not the data.
