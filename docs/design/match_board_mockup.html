ANNOTATIONS

1
Foe deck — opponent's draw pile on their side of the felt, card backs edged in their crest red. Replaces the single shared deck.
2
Your deck — mirrored on your side, gold-edged badge. Spy's Monocle shows your next draw peeking from underneath. Empty pile: stack disappears, badge turns red "0".
3
Opponent panel — name, archetype, hand count, Fury pips, race meter. Red keyline ties panel ↔ their deck.
4
Player panel — same anatomy, gold keyline. RACE TO ZERO: the bar fills toward WIN as cards are shed (hand + deck). Fill = cards shed ÷ starting total (10 here): both sides have shed 4, so both bars sit at 40%. Identical data renders identically; gold encodes progress, never ownership.
5
Phase ribbon — secondary echo only; the authoritative phase lives in the action zone (9). Sage your-attack / red defend / tan waiting.
6
Trump card — on the felt at the table's edge with its rule spelled out ("Hearts beat any other suit"). Trump changes flip this card with a flash and a log entry.
7
Removed pile — physically OFF the felt: these cards are out of the game. Top card face-up and ash-toned so the pile can't be misread as a recoverable discard.
8
Bout slots — felt inlays with ATTACK/DEFENSE role flags; defense overlaps ≤35% so the attack's rank, name and ability stay readable. +2 chip = rank bonus (Conquer); the 7♥ that answered it wears a ♥ TRUMP flag and red rim — the "why did my Ace lose" moment is self-explaining.
9
Action zone — phase line, bout counter with cap consequence ("tie → prestige"), and the single commit button: END BOUT / TAKE CARDS / disabled. One primary glow rule: hand-card glow marks options, button color marks the commit.
10
Run bar — prestige with visible maximum (8/10), florins, venue, collection size, auto toggle, menu (concede/settings).
11
Table talk log — last 5 actions, newest gold. Replaces the 1.2s toast as the record of ability triggers; expandable to full history.
12
Tooltip — anchored to the hovered card, arrow touching it; cost phrase tinted to match the resource pips, which pulse (3 Intel highlighted) while hovered. Flips above/below to avoid covering the bout.

DEFENDER'S PUNISH (TAKE CARDS)

Undefended attacks tilt red; the button reads TAKE 3 CARDS. On press, every bout card flies to the defender's hand, their race meter drains backwards with a thud, and the log records "Berto eats 3." TAKE 3 CARDS

MATCH END

Full-board dim; center panel: "BERTO IL RATTO IS THE FOOL" / "YOU ARE THE FOOL," prestige hearts animate the loss, then CONTINUE. Bout-cap tie: "THE TABLE TIRES — prestige decides," meters compared center-screen.

OPPONENT ACTING

A back lifts from Berto's fan, hovers 300ms over the target slot, flips on landing. Their hand count and race meter tick at the moment of the flip — numbers never change invisibly.

EMPTY DECK / LAST CARD

Empty pile: stack art gone, badge red "0," label dims. A player on their final card gets a subtle gold rim on their panel — the whole table can feel the match point.

Scaling strategy: the felt and bout scale within a 16:10 safe area; panels, piles and the action zone anchor to corners; on 16:9 the wood margins absorb the difference, never the felt. Reading surfaces (ability strips, labels, log, tooltips) are ≥12px at the 1280-wide reference; micro-flags (role/trump/WIN caps) are ≥10px display capitals. The board scales proportionally above reference. Motion rules: ① fully defended bout → all cards fly to REMOVED, both race meters tick up; ② defender eats → cards fly to their hand, their meter drains back; ③ forced discards visibly slide under the owner's deck (recycled, not removed); ④ trump change flips the trump card with a gold flash + log entry; ⑤ deck empty → badge pulses red once; ⑥ only one element glows gold at a time — options in hand, or the hovered card, never both at full intensity.
