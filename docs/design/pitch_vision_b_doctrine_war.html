The Core Idea

Durak with abilities. Your deck is your character build.

Every card still has a suit and rank — Durak combat resolves the same way. But each card also has an Ability that triggers at specific moments: when you attack, when you defend, or passively from your hand. Build around archetypes whose abilities synergize, creating emergent strategies that transform how Durak plays.

👁
The Schemer
"Knowledge is the sharpest blade."
Card draw, deck manipulation, information warfare. Schemers see more cards, cycle faster, and always know what's coming. They win through inevitability — grinding opponents down with card advantage and perfect information.
Draw Scry Reveal Cycle Mill
⚔
The Brute
"Overwhelming force needs no subtlety."
Raw numbers, forced pick-ups, relentless pressure. Brutes pump their cards' effective ranks, attack with multiple cards, and punish failed defenses. They win by making every bout a losing proposition for the defender.
+Rank Multi-attack Punish Force pickup Overwhelm
🃏
The Trickster
"The rules are just suggestions."
Trump manipulation, suit-shifting, rule-bending. Tricksters change the trump suit mid-bout, make cards count as different suits, and invalidate the opponent's carefully planned defense. They win by making the game play by their rules.
Trump shift Wild suit Redirect Nullify Transmute
💰
The Hoarder
"The one with the most cards wins... eventually."
Benefits from large hands, long games, and attrition. Hoarders gain power the more cards they hold, turn failed defenses (picking up cards) into advantages, and outlast opponents. They win by flipping Durak's core rule on its head.
Hand size Attrition On pickup Stockpile Endgame

ENEMY ROSTER

Enemies Have Archetypes Too

Learn their doctrine. Counter-draft. Adapt.

💪
Rocco the Enforcer
Pure Brute • Act 2
16-card deck loaded with +rank cards. Opens with his heaviest hitters. Punishes failed defenses with extra pickups. Crumbles if you change trump or survive the initial onslaught.
Threat: Overwhelming early pressure
👻
La Strega
Pure Trickster • Act 3
14-card deck. Changes trump constantly. Every defense shifts the rules. Your carefully-hoarded trump cards become dead weight mid-bout. Beatable with raw numbers or fast cycling.
Threat: Invalidates trump-dependent builds
🕵
The Cardinal
Schemer • Act 4
12-card deck. Sees your hand, knows your draws, always has the perfect counter. Never attacks recklessly. Outpaces you in card advantage. Beat by going faster or going bigger.
Threat: Perfect information, perfect responses
👑
Il Doge
Brute / Schemer Hybrid • Act 5 Boss
20-card deck with the best of both worlds. Scouts your hand then lands devastating attacks at your weakest point. Has unique legendary cards: "Decree" (change trump + draw 3) and "Conscription" (force you to pick up 4).
Threat: No single weakness. Requires a refined build.

DESIGN SUMMARY

Why This Vision Works

Strategic Identity
"I'm a Schemer with Trickster splash" is a fundamentally different experience than "Pure Brute." Every run is a new character.
One More Run
"What if I go Hoarder with the Bottomless Purse relic? I could hold 12 cards and make The Miser's passive insane..."
Durak Depth
Defending isn't just "play higher." It's "play THIS card because its defense trigger draws me 2." Attack isn't just aggression — it's information warfare.
Enemy Variety
Each enemy archetype creates a distinct puzzle. You learn the roster, counter-draft, adapt mid-run. No two fights feel the same.
Deck Tension
Adding a powerful Brute card to your Schemer deck is tempting — but it dilutes synergy AND slows your cycle. Real agony on every reward screen.
Growth Path
Start with 2 archetypes, expand to 4. Start with simple abilities, unlock complex ones. The game grows with the player.
"In the end, it's not about the cards in your hand — it's about the doctrine behind them." — The Doctrine War design philosophy
