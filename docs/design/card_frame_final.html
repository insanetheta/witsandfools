In hand (compact)

The drawer is collapsed: rank + suit (with the faint gold engraving behind), name, and the colour-coded ability label. Clean at hand size on the felt. Hover to preview the detail slide-up (this is the hover/tap detail view).

Reward / Deck / Detail (drawer open)

Exactly the same frame & elements as B — the drawer is simply open by default: the image recedes to the top and the full ability text (plus flavour) shows in the text box, like A's body panel but in B's language. The hover/tap detail view animates into this same state.

States (compact)

Playable = gold glow · trump defense = red tab at the foot · rank bonus = red coin top-right. Same on the open view.

One component, two presentations: the only difference between the hand card and the reward/deck/detail card is whether the drawer is open. In Unity: CardView renders the B frame + collapsed drawer; the reward/deck builders spawn the same CardView with open=true; and hover/tap (pointer or touch) toggles open with the slide-up animation. Body text + flavour come from the card definition.
